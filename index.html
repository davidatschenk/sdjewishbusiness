About

An open room, once in a while, for people who build things

San Diego Jewish Business Networking is an informal gathering of Jewish and Jewish-affiliated business owners, professionals, and entrepreneurs from across the county. There is no membership, no dues, and no pitch from the front of the room. People show up, order a drink, and talk. Some come to find a vendor or a client. Some come to compare notes with someone in the same seat. Most come back.

Everyone is welcome, at every stage and from every industry, Jewish and Jewish-affiliated alike.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who can attend?

Any Jewish or Jewish-affiliated business owner, professional, or entrepreneur in the San Diego area. Every industry, every stage, from someone with an idea to someone running a company of two hundred people.

Do I need to RSVP?

No. Just show up. If you would like a reminder before each event, use the contact form to get on the email list.

Is there a cost?

There is no cost to attend and no membership fee. You buy your own food and drinks.

How often do you meet?

Every few months, usually on a weekday evening. Upcoming dates are always posted at the top of this page.

What actually happens at one of these?

Drinks and conversation. No timed rotations, no speaker, no thirty-second introductions around the table. Come when you can, stay as long as you like.

Is this a religious event?

No. It is a business networking group for Jewish professionals. There is no service, no program, and no expectation about observance. No food is served. However, please let us know if any accommodations are required.

Can I bring a colleague or a friend?

Yes, please do. Word of mouth is how the group has grown.

I would like to host or sponsor an event.

Great. Send a note through the contact form and we will talk.
